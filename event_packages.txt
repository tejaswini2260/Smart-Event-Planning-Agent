# EVENT KNOWLEDGE BASE (SAMPLE DATA)

IMPORTANT: Every price, package and guideline in this file is an ILLUSTRATIVE ESTIMATE written for an academic project. These are NOT real offers, quotations or availability from any actual vendor. Always collect written quotations from real vendors.

## Wedding Packages
Basic wedding package (illustrative, about INR 3,00,000 to 6,00,000 for 100 to 200 guests): simple venue decoration with fresh and artificial flowers, a vegetarian buffet, one photographer, a sound system and a small stage. Standard wedding package (illustrative, about INR 6,00,000 to 12,00,000): themed stage and entrance, welcome drinks, two-cuisine buffet, photo and video team, DJ or live music, bridal room set-up. Premium wedding package (illustrative, above INR 12,00,000): custom floral design, multi-cuisine live counters, cinematic video, pre-wedding shoot, sangeet performances, guest hospitality desk. Wedding events usually include ceremony rituals, family photography, dinner and music, and need the longest planning lead time, typically 3 to 6 months.

## Birthday Party Packages
Kids birthday package (illustrative, about INR 25,000 to 60,000 for 30 to 60 guests): balloon decoration, themed backdrop, cake table, games host, snacks and juice menu, return gifts. Teen and adult birthday package (illustrative, about INR 50,000 to 1,50,000): theme decoration, DJ or playlist set-up, buffet or finger-food menu, photo booth corner. Milestone birthdays such as 50th or 60th often add a memory slideshow, a family dinner and a small stage for speeches. Birthday planning usually needs 3 to 6 weeks.

## Engagement and Anniversary Packages
Engagement package (illustrative, about INR 1,50,000 to 4,00,000 for 80 to 150 guests): ring ceremony stage, floral backdrop, welcome drinks, dinner buffet, photography and a short music programme. Anniversary package (illustrative, about INR 60,000 to 2,50,000 for 40 to 120 guests): candle-lit or fairy-light decoration, cake cutting, memory slideshow, dinner and soft music or a small live act. Silver and golden jubilee anniversaries often include family performances and a tribute video. Engagement planning usually needs 2 to 3 months, anniversaries 3 to 6 weeks.

## Naming Ceremony and Corporate Packages
Naming ceremony package (illustrative, about INR 40,000 to 1,20,000 for 40 to 100 guests): traditional decoration, ritual area for the family priest or elders, a vegetarian lunch, a photographer and return gifts. Corporate event package (illustrative, about INR 1,00,000 to 5,00,000 for 50 to 200 attendees): registration desk, stage and projector or LED screen, microphones, tea and lunch service, branded backdrop, team activity or awards segment. Corporate events depend heavily on audio-visual equipment and punctual schedules.

## Decoration Themes
Royal pink and gold: draped fabric, gold accents, rose and peony arrangements, suited to weddings and engagements. Rustic floral: wooden elements, wildflowers, jute and fairy lights, suited to outdoor or garden venues. Modern minimal: white and pastel palette, geometric backdrops and clean lighting, suited to anniversaries and corporate events. Traditional marigold: marigold garlands, brass lamps and rangoli, suited to naming ceremonies and ritual-heavy weddings. Kids fantasy: balloon arches, cartoon-inspired backdrops, bright colours and play corners for birthdays. Decoration typically takes 10 to 20 percent of the total budget; floral-heavy themes cost more than fabric and light-based themes.

## Catering Options
Vegetarian buffet (illustrative, about INR 400 to 800 per guest): starters, two main-course dishes, rice or breads, dal, dessert. Non-vegetarian buffet (illustrative, about INR 600 to 1,100 per guest): adds one or two chicken or mutton dishes and usually a larger dessert spread. Live counters such as chaat, pasta or dosa raise the per-guest cost but improve guest experience. Jain and vegan menus need a separate kitchen set-up and ingredient planning. Plan food for about 5 to 10 percent more guests than the confirmed count and finalise the menu with the caterer at least one week ahead. Catering usually takes 30 to 45 percent of the total budget.

## Photography and Entertainment
Photography options (illustrative): one photographer for small events (about INR 15,000 to 30,000), photo and video team for medium events (about INR 40,000 to 90,000), cinematic coverage with drone for large weddings (above INR 1,00,000). Entertainment options (illustrative): playlist and sound system (about INR 8,000 to 20,000), DJ (about INR 20,000 to 60,000), live band or singer (about INR 40,000 to 1,50,000), games host for kids parties (about INR 5,000 to 15,000). Confirm sound limits and permitted end times with the venue.

## Venue Capacity Considerations
Seating guideline: allow about 10 to 12 square feet per guest for theatre or buffet standing and about 15 to 18 square feet per guest for banquet seating with tables. Keep the guest count at or below about 90 percent of the stated venue capacity so that guests, caterers and decorators can move comfortably. If guests exceed capacity, either choose a larger venue, add a second area such as a lawn or terrace, or reduce the guest list. A venue that is far larger than needed can waste budget and make the event feel empty. Also check parking (roughly one vehicle space for every four guests), power backup, restrooms, a changing room and whether outside caterers are allowed.

## Budget Planning Guidelines
A common planning split for social events (illustrative): venue 20 to 30 percent, catering 30 to 45 percent, decoration 10 to 17 percent, photography 7 to 12 percent, entertainment 5 to 12 percent and miscellaneous 5 to 8 percent. Keep miscellaneous funds for transport, tips, licences and unexpected costs. Catering depends mostly on guest count, so a higher guest count with a fixed budget quickly creates a shortfall. If the budget is too low, reduce the guest list, simplify the menu, choose light-based decoration or shorten the programme before reducing safety or food quality.

## Event Preparation Timelines
Typical preparation timeline (illustrative): 3 months before - fix budget, guest list and date, shortlist venues. 2 months before - request quotations from at least three vendors for catering, decoration and photography, choose a theme. 1 month before - send invitations, finalise menu and decoration plan, book entertainment. 2 weeks before - prepare the run-sheet, confirm outfits and transport. 1 week before - confirm the final guest count with caterer and venue. 1 to 3 days before - reconfirm all vendors and prepare an emergency contact list. Event day - assign a coordinator, follow the schedule and keep a buffer of 15 minutes between segments.

## Safety and Logistics Tips
Keep fire exits clear and visible, avoid blocking aisles with decoration, and check that electrical load suits lighting and sound equipment. For outdoor events plan a weather backup such as a tent or an indoor hall. Arrange drinking water, first-aid supplies and a designated person for elderly guests. Plan about one service staff member for every 25 guests at buffet events. Arrange guest parking and clear directions, and share the venue contact person with all helpers.
