
SMART EVENT PLANNING KNOWLEDGE BASE

PACKAGE 1: TRADITIONAL WEDDING
Suitable for: Traditional Maharashtrian weddings.
Possible services: Stage decoration, floral entrance, seating arrangement, catering coordination and photography coordination.
Planning notes: Confirm guest count, venue capacity, ceremony requirements, decoration preferences and budget before finalizing a package.

PACKAGE 2: BIRTHDAY CELEBRATION
Suitable for: Children's and adults' birthday parties.
Possible services: Balloon decoration, themed backdrop, cake table, lighting and entertainment coordination.
Planning notes: Ask about the age group, selected theme, number of guests, venue and available budget.

PACKAGE 3: HALDI CEREMONY
Suitable for: Traditional Haldi functions.
Possible services: Yellow floral decoration, seating backdrop, traditional props and photo area.
Planning notes: Confirm the ceremony schedule, available space, decoration materials and cleanup requirements.

PACKAGE 4: ENGAGEMENT CEREMONY
Suitable for: Sakharpuda and ring ceremonies.
Possible services: Stage backdrop, floral arrangements, seating and photography coordination.
Planning notes: Confirm the ceremony style, guest count, venue and stage requirements.

BUDGET PLANNING GUIDELINES
1. Ask for the total budget and guest count.
2. Consider venue, decoration, catering, photography and miscellaneous expenses.
3. Keep some budget for unexpected costs.
4. Treat all sample costs as estimates until verified quotes are available.
5. Do not claim that any vendor is booked or available without confirmation.

VENUE PLANNING GUIDELINES
1. Compare guest count against the venue's stated capacity.
2. Check seating, parking, accessibility, electricity and facilities.
3. Confirm venue availability directly with the venue provider.
4. Do not infer actual availability from capacity information alone.
